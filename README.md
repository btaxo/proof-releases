# Proof — Releases

Download the **Proof** desktop app and read its release notes here.

**[⬇︎ Download Proof for Mac](https://github.com/btaxo/proof-releases/releases/latest/download/Proof.dmg)**  ·  **[Install guide & how to get an AI key](https://btaxo.github.io/proof-releases/)**

Proof helps you build, tailor, and track job applications — on your own computer. Your library and applications are stored on your Mac; when you use an AI feature, the relevant text is sent to Anthropic's Claude with your own key to generate a response, and nothing else is shared.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) running macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer. Intel Mac, Windows, and Linux builds are planned.
