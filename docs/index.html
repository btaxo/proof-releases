Proof.

Build, tailor, and track job applications — on your own computer.

Download for Mac

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) running macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer. Intel Mac, Windows, and Linux builds are planned.

  1. Download Proof.dmg and open it.
  2. Drag Proof into the Applications folder.
  3. Open Proof from Applications — it will ask for an AI key the first time. Here's how to get one.
Private by design. Your library and applications are stored on your own computer — nothing is uploaded to us. When you use an AI feature, the relevant text is sent to Anthropic with your own key to generate a response. Once a day, Proof also checks GitHub for a newer version. That check sends nothing about you or your data, and you can turn it off in Settings.
Proof is a small, independently made app.
